Legal Document Analyzer
A powerful AI-driven tool designed to parse, analyze, and summarize complex legal contracts and documents. This application leverages Natural Language Processing (NLP) to identify key clauses, detect potential risks, and provide high-level summaries to streamline legal review processes.

🚀 Features
Clause Extraction: Automatically identifies standard clauses (e.g., Indemnification, Termination, Governing Law).

Risk Assessment: Highlights non-standard or aggressive language that may require human legal review.

Entity Recognition: Detects parties, dates, jurisdictions, and monetary values.

Summarization: Generates concise executive summaries of long-form contracts.

Multi-format Support: Processes PDF, DOCX, and TXT files.

🛠️ Architecture
The system follows a modular pipeline:

OCR/Parsing: Extracts raw text from uploaded files.
